# TULPAR Brand Guide

## Brand Identity

TULPAR is a modern autonomous systems platform focused on modularity, reliability and scalability.

The visual identity reflects engineering precision, simplicity and technological excellence.

---

## Brand Values

- Professional
- Reliable
- Modular
- Innovative
- AI Ready
- Open Architecture

---

## Logo

The logo combines a stylized "T" with abstract wings, representing freedom, technology and autonomous movement.

The logo must remain simple, scalable and recognizable.
